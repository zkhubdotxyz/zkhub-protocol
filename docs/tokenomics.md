# Tokenomics

**Status: proposed design, not final. No token contract has been announced.** No supply, price, allocation percentage or date has been fixed. Figures will be published only when decided, together with the official contract address.

## Purpose of a token

A ZKHUB token, if launched, would coordinate access and incentives rather than exist as a standalone product. The candidate utilities are:

| Utility | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| Launch access | Staking weights priority in sealed-batch launches |
| Fee discounts | Reduced fees for stakers on launchpad and swap routing |
| Deeper forensics | Higher scan limits and trace depth, with a free tier for everyone |
| Governance | Votes on curation, risk thresholds and fee splits |
| Prover staking | Securing the prover and relayer set once settlement ships |
| Launch bonds | Creators post a bond, forfeited on a proven bundle or rug |

## Structure principles

- **Fairness first.** The token should not launch through the same mechanisms the product criticises.
- **Transparent vesting.** Any team or ecosystem allocation is locked and vested on-chain, with the schedule public.
- **No hidden supply.** Supply, mint authority and lock contracts are verifiable on the explorer.
- **Utility before speculation.** Features that use the token ship, or are labelled planned, before it is promoted.

## Categories under consideration

Allocation across these categories is undecided and will be stated as exact figures at announcement:

- Community and fair-launch distribution
- Liquidity
- Ecosystem and grants
- Team and contributors, with vesting
- Treasury

## Fees

A portion of launchpad and swap fees is intended to flow to stakers. The split is a governance topic and is not fixed.

## Official contract

The only official address will appear in the bar at the top of [zkhub.xyz](https://zkhub.xyz) and in the [README](../README.md). Anything else is not ZKHUB.
