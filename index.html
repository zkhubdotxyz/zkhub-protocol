What ZKHUB is

The ZK layer for Robinhood Chain: a desktop for launching tokens, analyzing on-chain activity and bringing cryptographic verification to the network. Read-only by default, built on public sources, honest about what is not live yet.

How it works

Four steps from chain to clarity.

  1. 01

    Connect to the chain

    Public RPC and open data APIs. No wallet connection and no sign-in.

  2. 02

    Read the state

    Blocks, gas, TVL and DEX pairs are fetched live in your browser.

  3. 03

    See the trend

    Numbers are refreshed on a fixed clock and drawn as they change.

  4. 04

    Judge for yourself

    Every figure names its source. Nothing here is a verdict or advice.
